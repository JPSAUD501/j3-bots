# J3 Bots 0.5.0

**Draft.** The notes for the 0.5.0 release, kept here while it is prepared. The release workflow adds the install section and the Android certificate's digest.

What your bots say to each other now has a place of its own: a read-only conversation for each pair of bots, outside the one you have with each bot. Ask a bot for a team and it makes the teammates, starts a room with them and gets them going. Events that only report something, like a message to a teammate or a routine created, are one small line instead of a card. And the first run moves as one piece, on the web and on Android. It still pins T3 Code 0.1.11.

How it reads is in [Experience: one bot hands work to another](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/blob/v0.5.0/docs/product/experience.md#one-bot-hands-work-to-another) and [several bots, one goal](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/blob/v0.5.0/docs/product/experience.md#several-bots-one-goal).

## Updating

This release speaks **protocol 9**. A 0.4 app and a 0.5 server, or the other way round, show each other as incompatible, with a screen that says which side is behind. Update the server and every app together. Updates are served outside the protocol, so a 0.4 app or server can still update the other side across the change.

- **The server:** from any device, in Settings → About and updates, or `j3-bots update` on its machine. Either copies the databases into a snapshot first, then runs this release's one migration, 0031: it lists every message your bots already sent each other in their pair's conversation, in the order they were sent. Nothing is moved or rewritten.
- **The desktop app:** the AppImage and the Windows install update themselves, once the download matches the release's signature. The deb and the Mac build link to this release.
- **The web client** comes with the server.
- **The Android app** updates itself.

### What changes on updating

- **Messages between bots leave your conversations.** In each bot's conversation, a run of them is one line ("Talked with Wren · 3 messages") that opens the pair's conversation. The messages from before the update are there too.
- **A message between bots never notifies you,** urgent or not, never makes a conversation unread, and is never a row's last line in the roster. A bot that needs you tells you itself. On Android, the "Urgent messages to teammates" channel gets nothing new.
- **Cards that only reported something are lines:** a teammate message, a routine created, changed, paused, resumed or deleted, a routine J3 Bots paused, and the name a bot saved for you. Anything you act on (a question, an approval, a login, a browser step, a connector) is still a card.
- **Only your own word makes a bot build a team.** The primary bot still creates bots, but now only in a turn your message or your answer started, at most five for one request.

### Rolling back

`j3-bots update --rollback` to the 0.4 release you updated from puts back the databases from the snapshot:

- the bots and rooms made since the update are lost, with the rest of the database; a made bot's folder stays on disk, as every bot's files do;
- messages between bots show in the bots' own conversations again, as they did in 0.4.0;
- your apps stay on 0.5.0 and show the server as behind: reinstall the 0.4.0 apps to keep using it.

Don't start 0.4.0 by hand on the updated data: it refuses a database that has migration 0031, and says to roll back.

## What's new

- **A conversation for each pair of bots** ([#251](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/251), [#252](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/252), [#256](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/256), [#259](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/259)). Two bots that wrote each other get one conversation, "Scout and Wren", on the web, the desktop and Android. It reads like a room: each bot's name over its messages, its face beside the last one, the time between quiet stretches, and a mark on a message that was urgent, or never reached the other bot. You read it, and can't write in it: a **Read-only** mark stands where the composer would be. Its menu has **Talk to Scout**, **Talk to Wren** and **Start a room with both**, which opens the room form with both bots in it.
- **"Between bots" in the roster.** After your bots and rooms, a section lists each pair, latest first: both faces, "Scout and Wren", the last message with who wrote it, and its time. These rows are never unread. A pair shows only while both bots are in the list, neither hidden nor deleted. Search finds a pair by either bot's name, a hidden bot's too. A bot's details list its teammates, a deleted one included, and each opens their conversation.
- **Run lines.** In a bot's own conversation, messages with teammates that follow each other are one line: "Wrote to Wren", "Wren wrote", "Talked with Wren · 3 messages", with "1 not delivered" when one didn't arrive. Tapping it opens the pair's conversation at the first message of the run. A run with several teammates ("Talked with Wren and Pip · 4 messages") first asks which conversation to open.
- **Bots build a team on your word** ([#254](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/254), [#260](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/260)). Tell your primary bot something like "make me a support group, several bots". In that one turn it makes the teammates it lacks, starts a room with them as its host, and posts a kickoff: what the room is for, who owns which part, and who opens. The members answer one at a time, each seeing the replies before it, and the bot tells you in its conversation what it set up. A bot made for the room opens there, with no hello in its own conversation. A routine, another bot, or something a bot read never starts a team. Any bot can post in a room it is a member of from its own conversation, and a room's Stop now ends that too.
- **Made bots run on their maker's model** ([#260](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/260)). A bot another bot creates gets its maker's provider and model while that model is offered and its provider is ready, and the server's default otherwise. Before, it always got the default, so a Codex bot's teammates could run on Claude Code.
- **The room's notices open it** ([#254](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/254), [#260](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/260)). "Scout started Support" in the maker's conversation, and "Made by Scout for Support" in each new bot's, are lines that open the room. They name the room as it is called now, and say "a room" once it is deleted.
- **Rooms read like a pair's conversation** ([#256](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/256), [#259](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/259)). Each bot's name stands over its run of messages, with its face beside the last. The header shows three members' faces and "+2" for the rest. A deleted bot's messages keep their place, as "A deleted bot".
- **One line for what only happened** ([#253](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/253)). A quiet, centred line as tall as a time row: "Routine Morning digest created", "3 routines created", "Scout saved your name: Sam". Routine changes by the same bot in a row join into one line, and an edit right after a creation folds into it. Tapping a line opens what it is about: the routine, a list of the routines, or the pair's conversation. A screen reader reads the whole sentence with its mark.
- **The first run moves as one** ([#255](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/255)). The web and Android now share the same timings. The bot you are making is one bot from the welcome to its conversation: it lifts out of the welcome's cast to the top of the steps, goes back on Back, and after its arrival flies into the header of its conversation. Steps slide in from the side they come from, the cast floats, and typing a name no longer restarts the bot. With reduced motion, steps and the bot crossfade and nothing travels.

## Known limits

**Not tried for real yet:**

- **A physical phone.** Android ran on an emulator. The first run's motion was not measured on a real mid-range phone, and TalkBack's speech was not recorded for the new lines and conversations.
- **The bot flying into its header** on the web was recorded only in Chromium, not in Firefox or Safari. A browser without view transitions just opens the conversation.
- **The desktop app** was not run for the new screens; it draws the web client.
- **On Android**, opening a pair's conversation at a run far back in a long conversation, and the pair's conversation on a tablet, were not seen.
- **Many busy pairs.** How the roster's pairs keep up with many bots writing each other was reasoned, not measured.
- **The notice lines and made bots' models** ([#260](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/260)) were shot and evaluated before their last rebase; only the checks ran after it.

**Limits of this version:**

- **A Stop can lose a race with `create_bot`** ([#257](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/issues/257)). Between your Stop and the engine ending the turn, the bot can still create a bot or start a room.
- **Stopping a room doesn't reach another room** ([#258](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/issues/258)). If a member's work in room A posted in room B, stopping A leaves B's members to answer. B's own limit on bot turns, or a Stop of B, ends it.
- **Asking for a team while the bot is working** doesn't count: a message that lands in a running turn isn't proven to be yours to the bot. It says so, and asks you to ask again once the turn is over.
- **The roster's "Made by Scout for Support"** opens the new bot's conversation, not the room; the line in that conversation opens the room.
- **On the web, on a phone,** Back from a pair's conversation goes to the roster. The next and previous conversation shortcuts skip pairs, and a screen reader isn't told when a pair's conversation gets a new message.
- **On Android**, a tap right after a sheet closes sometimes does nothing.
- **The first run's edges:** Back pressed while the bot is being carried starts it home from its resting place, not from where it is; on Android, Back with the keyboard open carries it to where the cast stood before, and the system Back during the flight into the header lets it land first.
- **For contributors:** on GitHub's Windows runner, several unrelated server tests sometimes time out together in the same minute, and the next run passes. Its cause is not captured yet ([#248](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/issues/248)).

Everything listed under "Known limits" in the [0.4.0](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/releases/tag/v0.4.0) notes still applies.
