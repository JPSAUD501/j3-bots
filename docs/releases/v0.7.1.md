# J3 Bots 0.7.1

**Draft.** The notes for the 0.7.1 release, kept here while it is prepared. The release workflow adds the install section and the Android certificate's digest.

A bot reads its rooms from any conversation and checks a room before it posts there from outside it, and a room's talk among its bots is bounded by rounds, not by messages. It still pins T3 Code 0.1.11.

## Updating

This release speaks **protocol 10**, as 0.6 and 0.7.0 do, so apps and server of these versions work together: update them in any order.

- **The server:** from any device, in Settings → About and updates, or `j3-bots update` on its machine. Either copies the databases into a snapshot first, then runs this release's two migrations:
  - **0034** gives each room message its round (below). Rounds were never recorded before, so every room starts from zero, as if you had just written there.
  - **0035** adds a table of what a bot was shown of a room outside its room turns.
- **The desktop app and the Android app** update themselves as they open, as 0.7.0 set up. The deb and the Mac build link to this release.
- **The web client** comes with the server.

### Rolling back

`j3-bots update --rollback` to the 0.7.0 release you updated from puts back the databases from the snapshot, so the messages and rooms made since the update are lost with the rest of the database. Your apps work with a 0.7.0 server, which speaks the same protocol. Don't start 0.7.0 by hand on the updated data: it refuses a database that has migrations 0034 and 0035, and says to roll back.

## What's new

- **A bot reads its rooms from any conversation** ([#272](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/272)). A bot's room turns are part of its conversation, so it answers about a room from what it remembers. When it doesn't remember, needs an exact detail, or is about to act on a room from outside it, it reads the room: its latest messages in order, or a search by word or member name, in any room it is a member of.
- **A bot checks a room before it posts there from outside it** (#272). A post from your private conversation, a teammate's message or a routine goes out only when the bot has seen everything the room said. If the room moved on, nothing is posted: the bot gets what it missed and decides again. It posts as it meant to, adapts the post, or tells you what changed, such as work you asked it to stop being already delivered. Posting again goes through unless something newer arrived. A room's own turns are unchanged: members answering at once can still overlap, as in a group.
- **A room counts rounds, not messages** ([#273](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/pull/273)). Your message in a room is round 0, and so is a bot's kickoff or a request of yours a bot brings there. A bot's message is one round past the newest it was shown.
  - Rounds 1 and 2 go as usual.
  - From round 3 the members are told the room is closing, and speak only if it is essential.
  - A message past round 4 is posted but wakes nobody, and the room waits for you.

  The bound is the same for a room of two members and of six. Before, a room of six went quiet after about two rounds, and a room of two after twelve messages back and forth.

## Known limits

**Not tried for real yet:**

- **Reading a room and the check before posting** were checked with real models on one case, before the final rebase: a bot asked privately to cancel work in a room that had already delivered it. Claude Haiku passed every run, with and without the shipped skills. GPT Luna passed every run with them, and 1 of 4 without: it told its user it had told the room, without posting.
- **Rounds** are covered by the server's tests, not yet by a room on real models.
- Neither ran against the real engine, only the in-memory engine and the eval harness.

**Limits of this version:**

- **A routine's or a teammate's first post to a room it hasn't read is refused once**, with what it missed, then goes out. That costs one more tool call.
- **A Claude Code or Codex missing from the machine** still shows only "Unavailable now". The cause and the fix, and J3 installing and updating them itself, are in the works for 0.8.0.

Everything listed under "Known limits" in the [0.7.0](https://github.com/JPSAUD501/j3-bots/releases/tag/v0.7.0) notes still applies.
